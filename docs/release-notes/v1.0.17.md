## Mackit 1.0.17

Three more interface languages, a detail sheet on double-click, and full paths inside the results table.

- **Russian, Spanish and French join the interface languages** — every screen is translated in all three, selectable in Settings or followed from the system language, and Russian count phrases follow Russian grammar (1 файл / 2 файла / 5 файлов).
- **Double-clicking a scan result opens its detail sheet** — the entry's full path, size, modification date and risk, with clean, show-in-Finder, copy-path and close actions.
- **Each scan result's path now lives inside its name column** — it moved out of its own narrow column, where it truncated to a fraction of its width, into the name column as a third line with the full path on hover, and the category filters moved from the window's sidebar into a draggable rail beside the table.

### Under the hood

- Count phrases pick their plural form per language through one `L10n.plural` helper — Russian takes its three integer forms, French counts zero with the singular — replacing the per-call-site one/other ternary, and `LocalizationTests` now checks all four forms' placeholder layouts.
