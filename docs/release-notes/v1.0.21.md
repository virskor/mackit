## Mackit 1.0.21

The tray panel is fully opaque and stops re-rendering while closed.

- **The tray panel no longer shows the wallpaper through its background** — the panel window paints its own opaque surface instead of the system's translucent popover chrome, and the panel follows light and dark appearance like every other surface.
- **The tray agent stops re-rendering while the panel is closed** — the hidden panel no longer re-lays-out on every monitoring tick, and the process ranking is sampled only while the panel is open, with a forced fresh sample taken right before it appears.

### Under the hood

- The tray panel is a borderless, non-activating `NSPanel` rather than an `NSPopover`: the popover's chrome is window-drawn material that samples the desktop behind the window, with no view to reach and nothing content, a layer or the window background can paint over, so the whole panel surface is painted by SwiftUI.
- A sampled profile of the running agent showed the SwiftUI layout engine as its top consumer: a hosted view re-lays-out its tree on every state change even when its window is ordered out, so the panel body collapses to a placeholder while hidden (`isPanelPresented`) and the all-process ranking is only sampled while presented.
- The transient popover's dismissal is reproduced explicitly: a global mouse-down monitor closes the panel on any click outside the app, clicks inside the panel or on the status item keep it open, and the panel never takes key focus (`nonactivatingPanel`, `becomesKeyOnlyIfNeeded`).
- The window's shadow derives from the hosting view's alpha, so it follows the rounded outline, and the corner radius is the shared `Theme.Radius.lg`.
